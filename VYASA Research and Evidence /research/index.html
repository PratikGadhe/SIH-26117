VYASA

Research & Evidence

Vision-augmented Yield & Agentic Synthesis Architecture

Sovereign, multimodal, agentic AI workbench for confidential industrial work.

Research conclusion: Local AI, RAG, agents, multimodal AI, enterprise governance and industrial AI are established capabilities. VYASA therefore does not claim these technologies individually as unique; its positioning is their combination and specialization for confidential industrial knowledge and engineering workflows.

Research Structure

01

Problem Statement

Confidential industrial documents, engineering diagrams and operational knowledge require strong control over data, infrastructure, processing and access.

02

System Architecture

React + Vite → FastAPI → LangGraph → Ollama → Qwen3 / Qwen3-VL, with RAG, PDF/OCR and SQLite/JWT/RBAC/audit.

03

Existing Solution Landscape

Enterprise AI: ChatGPT Enterprise, Microsoft Copilot, Claude. Self-hosted/local AI: Open WebUI, AnythingLLM, Dify.

04

Industrial AI Landscape

Siemens Industrial Copilot, AVEVA, Cognite and specialized engineering/P&ID intelligence illustrate the established industrial AI landscape.

05

Competitive Analysis

RAG, agentic workflows, multimodal AI, governance and local deployment are established capabilities across different product categories.

06

VYASA Differentiation

Sovereign + Multimodal + Industrial + Agentic, with a future pathway toward validated organizational intelligence.

07

Prototype Evidence

Local Qwen3/Qwen3-VL, LangGraph, ChromaDB RAG, PDF/OCR, image/PDF analysis, JWT, RBAC and audit logging have been validated.

08

Technical Stack

React + Vite, FastAPI, LangGraph, Ollama, Qwen3 4B, Qwen3-VL 4B, ChromaDB, Sentence Transformers, PyMuPDF, Tesseract, SQLite and Docker-oriented deployment.

09

Research Papers

Research areas include multimodal industrial AI, P&ID understanding, GraphRAG, engineering document intelligence and industrial knowledge graphs.

10

References

Official product documentation, industrial AI sources and academic research are listed below.
